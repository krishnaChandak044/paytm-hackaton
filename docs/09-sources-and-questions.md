## Claims to avoid

- “No setup at all”: the owner must confirm obligations; external accounts require consent and integration.
- “We know the merchant's cash”: Paytm-only data does not show cash, other providers or unconnected accounts.
- “A recurring transfer proves a bill”: amounts and due dates can change.
- “Paytm payment decline means stockout”: payment volume does not reveal inventory or cause.
- “We know missed gross profit”: item-level sales, costs and counterfactual demand are missing.
- “Peer twins prove the difference”: observational peers cannot establish causal effect without a credible study.
- “A ₹17,000 loan for six days costs ₹X”: no actual eligible lending offer or terms have been obtained.
- “4.9 crore merchants are available for this model”: registered count is not equivalent to active, consented, data-complete merchants.
- “Munim runs on the Soundbox today”: no general integration permission is established.

## Decisions Paytm and partners must answer

1. Which merchant transaction, refund, chargeback and settlement events can a hackathon/prototype access? Through which sandbox or approved API?
2. Which internal product can expose a private, merchant-only Munim card and notification? Can a Soundbox safely speak sensitive cash information?
3. Can a qualified partner obtain connected-account data under an Account Aggregator flow for this stated purpose? What are the actual consent and data-retention terms?
4. Which settlement options are offered to which merchant segments, and what are their actual costs and timing guarantees?
5. What is the approved path for filing a merchant settlement case and receiving status updates?
6. Are outbound merchant-to-supplier payments visible anywhere? If not, how should the recurring-obligation candidate be supplied without new daily work?
7. What merchant segments experience enough actionable cash timing problems to justify the service?
8. What does the owner consider a meaningful warning threshold and acceptable notification frequency?
9. Which languages and noisy-shop conditions must be supported first?
10. Which business outcomes can be measured without implying unseen sales or profit?

## Final scope discipline

The first credible Munim is **cash timing + verified settlement follow-up + merchant-approved options**. It earns the right to broaden only after merchant discovery, data access, calibration and field outcomes support doing so. This discipline is the product's trust advantage.
